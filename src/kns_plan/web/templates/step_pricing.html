2. Cost stack

How the brand price becomes a shelf price. Lines run top to bottom. A % of a base line adds a share of the brand price (fob), of the running total (running) or of an earlier line (its ID). A % of resulting price line is a margin on the next subtotal (wholesale = landed ÷ (1 − margins)). A subtotal closes a step; the last line must be the shelf price incl. VAT.
